Autoencoders with topological constraints


First idea: match persistence diagrams [Carrière et al., 2021] \[ \mathcal{L}_t=\sum_{\hdim\in\{0,1\}}\calw_2\bigl(\dgmrips{\hdim}(\pc),\dgmrips{\hdim}(\lpc)\bigr). \] $\rightarrow$ loses information on simplices involved in the cycles.

Topological Autoencoders [Moor et al., 2020]: \[ \begin{split} \mathcal{L}_t=\ltopoae{\hdim}= &\|A^\pc[\critical{\hdim}(\pc)]-A^\lpc[\critical{\hdim}(\pc)]\|_2^2 +\\ &\|A^\lpc[\critical{\hdim}(\lpc)]-A^\pc[\critical{\hdim}(\lpc)]\|_2^2 \end{split} \] where $\critical{\hdim}(\pc)$ are the critical edges of $\pc$
(create/destroy a positive $\PH{q}$ class, $q\leq\hdim$).

In practice, in [Moor et al., 2020], $\hdim=0$: \[ \begin{split} \mathcal{L}_t=\ltopoae{0}= &\|A^\pc[\critical{0}(\pc)]-A^\lpc[\critical{0}(\pc)]\|_2^2+\\ &\|A^\lpc[\critical{0}(\lpc)]-A^\pc[\critical{0}(\lpc)]\|_2^2 \end{split} \] $\rightarrow$ favors preservation of connected components.

Motivation


Rips filtration:

  • $\calo(2^n)$ simplices in total, $\calo(n^3)$ for the 2-skeleton
  • despite parallel implementations (CPU / GPU), still very expensive (time and memory)

Delaunay–Čech filtration:

  • restriction to the Delaunay complex: far fewer simplices, but low dimensionality only ($\ddim\lesssim10$)

Delaunay–Rips filtration:

  • restriction to the Delaunay complex: far fewer simplices, but low dimensionality only ($\ddim\lesssim10$)
  • unstable w.r.t. perturbations
  • but specific structure allowing optimizations for computing its PH

Theoretical guarantees


Rips approximation [Clémot et al., 2025b]
The $\hdim$-dimensional Rips and Delaunay–Rips persistence modules $\big(\hgf{\hdim}{\ripsn}{r}\big)_{r\geq0}$ and $\big(\hgf{\hdim}{\delripsn}{r}\big)_{r\geq0}$ are multiplicatively, strongly $\jung{\hdim+1}$-interleaved. As a corollary: \[ \bottle\left(\log\dgmdr{\hdim}(\pc),\log\dgmrips{\hdim}(\pc)\right)\leq\log\jung{\hdim+1}. \]


Instability upper bound [Clémot et al., 2025b]
Let $X$ and $Y$ be two point clouds. Then for any $\hdim\geq0$: \[ \bottle\left(\dgmdr{\hdim}(X),\dgmdr{\hdim}(Y)\right) \leq\left(\jung{\hdim+1}-1\right)\max\{\delta(X),\delta(Y)\} + 2\gromov(X,Y). \]

Why?

Jung's theorem [Jung, 1901]
Any compact set $K\subset\bbr^\ddim$ of diameter $\delta(K)$ has a minimum enclosing ball of radius $\meb(K)$ satisfying: \[\delta(K) \leq 2\meb(K) \leq \delta(K)\underbrace{\sqrt{\frac{2\ddim}{\ddim+1}}}_{\triangleq\jung{\ddim}}.\]
Homotopy equivalence [Bauer et al., 2017]
For any $r\geq0$, there are the following sequences of elementary collapses (hence homotopy equivalences): \[\cechf{r}(\pc)\collapses\delcechf{r}(\pc)\collapses\alphaf{r}(\pc).\]

Bifiltrations

Bifiltration (general definition)
A bifiltration is a family of topological spaces $(X_{r,s})_{(r,s)\in\bbr^2}$ satisfying: \[X_{r,s}\subseteq X_{r',s'}\text{ whenever } r\leq r'\text{ and }s\leq s'.\]
  • The set of bigrades $\bigrades(\sigma)\subset\bbr^2$ represents "when" a simplex is inserted
  • Unlike the single-parameter case: no analogue of the persistence diagram
  • Example of descriptor: multiparameter module approximation (MMA) [Loiseaux et al., 2025]

1-critical: $|\bigrades(\sigma)|=1$

multi-critical: $|\bigrades(\sigma)|$ can be $>1$

In the planar case where $L,X\subset\bbr^2$, for any $\hdim\in\bbn$: \[\bottle\left(\dgm{\hdim}(\flood(X,L)), \dgm{\hdim}(\alphaf{}(X))\right)\leq2\hausdo(X,L).\]

Stability


Stability (fixed landmarks) [Clémot et al., 2026]
Let $X,Y\subset\bbr^d$ be two point sets, and $\gamma_X$ and $\gamma_Y:\bbr^d\to\bbr$ two $\lipconst$-Lipschitz functions. With $L$ fixed: \[ \interl\left(\floodmodule(X,L,\gamma_X), \floodmodule(Y,L,\gamma_{Y})\right)\leq\max\left(\hausdo(X,Y),\lipconst\hausdo(X,Y)+\lVert\gamma_X-\gamma_Y\rVert_\infty\right). \]
+ refinements when $\gamma_X$ and $\gamma_Y$ are obtained by KDE or DTM

Conclusion: summary


Cycle-aware dimensionality reduction

  • generalization of the Topological Autoencoders formulation
  • specialized persistence algorithm for planar point sets

Delaunay–Rips filtration

  • filtration for low-dimensional point sets
  • characterization of the instabilities and performance evaluation on classification
  • specialized persistence algorithm

Sublevel Flood bifiltration

  • scalable approach for 2-parameter PH
  • theoretical and empirical behavior
  • GPU implementation

A few perspectives


Dimensionality reduction

  • Limits of metric-based filtrations (curse of dimensionality)
  • Use of spectral distances
  • Investigate more qualitative PH-based priors
  • Representation of higher-dimensional features

Multiparameter PH

  • Theoretical work on the Flood (bi)filtration
  • Accelerations for other families of bifiltrations: degree-Rips...
